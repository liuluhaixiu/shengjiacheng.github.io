## Overview

Cave Man is a horror game where players explore a dark cave. 
Inspired by the Video of 神秘园, I want to experience the psychological horror that explorers trapped deep in underground caves might go through — the terror of darkness, the unknown, and the gradual descent into the depths.

Expected to launch on Steam by the end of 2026.
