## Abstract

This paper presents a novel approach to geoprivacy protection through minimal image edits. Our method demonstrates that subtle, carefully crafted modifications to images can effectively obscure location information while preserving visual quality.

## Key Contributions

- A minimal-edit framework for geoprivacy protection
- Novel metric for measuring privacy-utility tradeoffs
- Extensive evaluation on real-world datasets
- State-of-the-art performance on privacy preservation benchmarks

## Method Overview

Our approach leverages adversarial perturbations combined with a learned privacy-preserving transformation to achieve strong protection against location inference attacks.
